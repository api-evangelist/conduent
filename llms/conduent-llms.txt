## Interfaces that exist but are not publicly specified

- **Healthcare interoperability (HL7 FHIR R4 / CMS-9115-F).** Conduent states it operates a
  "secure platform for members and 3rd party app developers to exchange patient claim data" with the
  CMS-mandated APIs, deployable in 10–12 weeks. The resulting Patient Access, Provider Directory,
  Drug Formulary, Provider Access, Payer-to-Payer and Prior Authorization surfaces are published by
  the contracting state Medicaid program under the state's own name and registration process, not by
  Conduent. See https://www.conduent.com/government-solutions/medicaid-public-health-solutions/medicaid-program-management/interoperability/
- **Knowledge Connection webhooks.** Conduent's learning platform ships eight documented webhook
  events (enrollment failed/completed/idle, learning activity failed/completed/idle, learner
  updated, assessment submitted), configured per portal and optionally managed through Zapier. Event
  names and trigger semantics are public; payload schemas, signing and retry behaviour are not.
  See https://help.knowledgeconnection.conduent.info/
- **Knowledge Connection Partner "Request Launch URL" API.** A contract-granted REST call returning a
  launch URL for a subscription/product. No public reference or base URL.
- **Standards consumed by Knowledge Connection:** IMS LTI 1.x/2.0, xAPI/TinCan (as an LRS client),
  SCORM 1.2 / SCORM 2004 / AICC, SAML 2.0 SSO.
