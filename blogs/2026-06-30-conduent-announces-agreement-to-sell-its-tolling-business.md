Conduent Bolsters Financial Position Tolling Agreement Follows Recently Announced Agreement to Sell Its Public Transit Business FLORHAM PARK, N.J., June 30, 2026 — Conduent Incorporated (Nasdaq: CNDT), a global technology-driven business solutions and services provider, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to sell its Tolling business (a division of Conduent Transportation ) to Quarterhill Inc. (TSX: QTRH) (OTCQX: QTRHF), a leading global provider of intelligent transportation system solutions.
